General #

0.0 # Purpose

  1. 0.0.1 # To ensure a process that is transparent, fair, and free from bias to select and send delegates to represent the Engineering Society and the Faculty of Applied Science and Engineering at nationwide student engineering conferences. Additionally, this policy sets out expectations of behaviour for delegates and the financial processes that are followed.

0.1 # Definitions

  1. 0.1.1 # “Skule™,” “Engineering Society” or “the Society” shall mean the University of Toronto Engineering Society.
  2. 0.1.2 # “Faculty” or “UofT Engineering” shall mean the University of Toronto Faculty of Applied Science and Engineering.
  3. 0.1.3 # “Director” shall mean the External Relations Director as defined in Bylaw 2, Section 1.25
  4. 0.1.4 # “President” shall mean the Engineering Society’s President as defined in Bylaw 1, Section 3.1
  5. 0.1.5 # “VP Finance” shall mean the Engineering Society’s Vice-President Finance as defined in Bylaw 1, Section 3.2
  6. 0.1.6 # “CFES” shall mean the Canadian Federation of Engineering Students
  7. 0.1.7 # “ESSCO” shall mean the Engineering Students Societies’ Council of Ontario

Conference Planning #

1.0 # Conference Schedule

  1. 1.0.1 # In transition, the outgoing Director should compile a list of conferences offered by the ESSCO and the CFES in the previous school year, along with the number of delegates sent, host school, method of transportation and finances incurred to the Society.
  2. 1.0.2 # With the aforementioned list and information of the dates and locations of the conferences offered by the ESSCO and the CFES for the upcoming academic year, the Director should meet with the President to discuss delegate numbers for each conference, considering conference location, cost, timing in the year, and experience at that conference in previous years.
  3. 1.0.3 # A proposed schedule of the number of delegates to send to each conference should be presented at the May Board of Directors Meeting.

1.1 # Conference Budgeting

  1. 1.1.1 # Anticipated funding requirements should be submitted through the External Relations budget in the June cycle.
  2. 1.1.2 # The budget should consider previous year expenditures when budgeting for airfare and other transportation, reflecting that these prices might vary throughout the year.
    1. 1.1.2.a # The Director shall work with the VP Finance to ensure the costs of attending conferences are in-line with the Society’s operating budget.

1.2 # External Calendar

  1. 1.2.1 # With the information of the conference schedule, the Director must update the publicly available External Calendar with their chosen Application Form Deadline, Interview Timelines and the Final Delegate Selection Date.
  2. 1.2.2 # The Final Delegate Selection Date must be three business days prior to the anticipated closing of the Registration Form.
  3. 1.2.3 # The interview period must be open for a week prior to the Final Delegate Selection Date.
  4. 1.2.4 # The Application Form Deadline must be four or more business days prior to the Final Delegate Selection Date, to allow for all selected Delegates to schedule their interviews.
  5. 1.2.5 # If a Conference moves their Registration deadline to be earlier than the anticipated closing date, the Director should modify all dates on the calendar as soon as reasonably possible.

1.3 # Financial Terms

  1. 1.3.1 # By default, the Society will try to reasonably cover all costs associated with conference attendance, including delegate fees and transportation costs.
  2. 1.3.2 # Should the Society determine that they cannot fully cover attendance costs for specific conferences, the Director will work with the President and the VP Finance to determine a reasonable portion of the conference costs to be covered by the selected delegates.
  3. 1.3.3 # All non-zero financial implications must be communicated to members in advertising, in the interview stage and confirmed upon being selected as a delegate.

Delegate Selection for Conferences #

2.0 # Eligibility

  1. 2.0.1 # Only members of the Society are eligible to apply as delegates.

2.1 # Application Process

  1. 2.1.1 # An application form, created by the Director or their Team, must be advertised on appropriate social media platforms, email lists, and other relevant communication channels at least 7 days before the application deadline.
  2. 2.1.2 # All interested delegates must fill out the application form for the conference they are interested in attending.
  3. 2.1.3 # The Director, and/or an appointed member of the External Relations team, will review all applications following the close of the application and shortlist candidates based on the applicants’ responses, including their goals, experience, and reasons for applying to the conference.
  4. 2.1.4 # Interviews, held by two or more appointed members of the External Relations team, will be held to further shortlist candidates, and to receive verbal confirmation to follow all code of conduct procedures if selected.
  5. 2.1.5 # The Director and all appointed members of the External Relations team involved in shortlisting and the final selection must disclose any potential Conflicts of Interest.
  6. 2.1.6 # Delegates will be notified of their application status on or before the Final Delegate Selection Date.
  7. 2.1.7 # Once selected, the Director must provide the official conference registration form to the Delegates with enough time for the Delegates to reasonably complete before the registration deadline.

Expectations of Delegates Attending Conferences #

3.0 # Delegate Contract & Code of Conduct

  1. 3.0.1 # The Director must produce a contract, with basic information about the conference, as well as code of conduct procedures all delegates must follow when under the jurisdiction of the conference as a representative of the Society. This includes all information in Section 3, alongside other guidelines on obligations, code of conduct, financial terms, reporting and enforcement and general provisions to attending a conference.
  2. 3.0.2 # The selected Delegates must sign and return the contract within two weeks of being selected as a Delegate.
  3. 3.0.3 # A $50.00 CAD deposit will be made to the Society to confirm the delegate’s commitment to the code of conduct. The method of payment is specified in the Delegate Contract.
  4. 3.0.4 # Any additional financial implications outlined in Section 1.4 will be paid to the Society with the same method as the deposit.

3.1 # Pre-Conference Briefings

  1. 3.1.1 # A group chat with all delegates, the Director, and any External Relations team members who are aiding in the logistics of the conference, shall be formed. The External Relations team will use the group chat to communicate with delegates, including gathering information to book travel. Delegates will also be able to ask questions to the External Relations team.
  2. 3.1.2 # A pre-conference briefing must be held within fourteen (14) days prior to the first day of the conference. This briefing will cover all topics related to the conference and how the delegates can prepare best, including detailed travel instructions and what to bring to be an efficient delegate.

3.2 # Post-Conference Debriefs

  1. 3.2.1 # A post-conference debrief must be held within fourteen (14) days following the final day of the conference. This briefing will cover how the conference went and delegates will share their contributions to the conference, personal takeaways, ideas, or lessons learned from attending the conference, and benefits on why the Society should continue sending delegates to this conference.
  2. 3.2.2 # Upon satisfactory completion of the Post-Conference Debrief as outlined in the Delegate Contract & Code of Conduct, the Society shall return the $50.00 CAD to the Delegate.
  3. 3.2.3 # The Director will present a summary of the debrief to the Society, commenting on the value of the conference and the takeaways made to improve the workings of the Society.

3.3 # Consequences of Non-Fulfilment

  1. 3.3.1 # If the Delegate fails to fulfill any obligation after signing the contract in Section 2.3, they will forfeit their $50.00 CAD deposit and will be restricted from selection for other Conferences offered by the Society.
  2. 3.3.2 # Non-fulfilment includes failure to pay any non-zero costs owed to the Society as outlined in Section 1.4
  3. 3.3.3 # Where a failure to fulfill an obligation results from illness, emergency, accessibility needs, or other circumstances beyond the Delegate's control, the Delegate should raise such circumstances as early as possible. The Director, along with the President, will review the circumstances on a case-by-case basis and reverse the decision stated in Section 2.6.1 if deemed reasonable.

Travel Guidelines #

4.0 # Air Transportation

  1. 4.0.1 # The Director, or an appointed member of the External Relations team, will be responsible for finding reasonable airfare to be purchased.
    1. 4.0.1.a # It is recommended that the flights be booked through any major online travel agency or directly through the airline. Google Flights should be used to compare airfare options. If a discount is provided by the conference, the discount should be used to see final airfare and compared to other flights. If cheaper, the flight should be booked with the provided discount.
    2. 4.0.1.b # Delegates should travel together when possible. If a Delegate wishes to arrange alternate travel plans, they must inform the Director and will be fully responsible for the costs.
  2. 4.0.2 # The ticket must state that the flight lands before check-in closes and must depart after checkout. If there are significantly cheaper flights (at least $150 cheaper round trip) that may impact Delegates’ participation in the conference, then the Director must consider the implications and trade-offs of choosing the cheaper flights.
  3. 4.0.3 # Generally, nonstop flights are preferred when available. For nonstop flights over 3 (three) hours, if connecting flights with layovers less than 3 (three) hours in each direction are cheaper than a nonstop itinerary, they should be booked (provided they follow 4.1.4).
  4. 4.0.4 # Connecting flights must be booked on the same ticket. Delegates shall not book connecting flights with separate tickets, as this does not protect onward travel in the case of delays or cancellations.
  5. 4.0.5 # Travel must be booked into the lowest economy fare that includes a standard-sized carry-on bag.
    1. 4.0.5.a # Delegates may purchase a standard-sized carry-on bag, or a checked-in bag if the fare does not include a carry-on bag. Only options chosen to be the cheapest will be eligible for reimbursement.
    2. 4.0.5.b # Delegates will not be reimbursed for purchases made over these guidelines without prior authorization, including, but not limited to: seat selection, checked baggage, premium seating, charged food and beverage, inflight entertainment (including WiFi), and travel insurance.
      1. 4.0.5.b.i # An exception may be made by the Director with approval from the President to purchase assigned seats or checked baggage for a longer-duration flight or conference.
  6. 4.0.6 # Travel will be reimbursed on a round-trip basis from the less expensive of either Toronto Pearson (YYZ) or Toronto City Centre (YTZ) airport. Should a delegate combine personal travel with the conference, they must retain a quote for the lowest cost of round-trip airfare from Toronto to the conference at the time of flight booking, and will be reimbursed for this amount.
  7. 4.0.7 # The Director should convene the delegation for a booking meeting, where transportation is booked together to ensure it follows this policy.

4.1 # Public Transport (Bus/Train)

  1. 4.1.1 # Public transport is an encouraged form of transport, especially to destinations within the Greater Golden Horseshoe region.
  2. 4.1.2 # Intercity buses and trains (except GO) will be booked in a group by the Director, or appointed member of the External Relations Team, at least 10 days prior to departure to access advance purchase fairs. Discounts provided by the conference should be used if they cheapen the cost.
  3. 4.1.3 # Delegates should travel on the same vehicle where possible.
  4. 4.1.4 # As GO tickets cannot be purchased in advance, delegates may pay for their own GO fares out-of-pocket and be reimbursed. For reimbursement, a receipt or equivalent proof of payment from all delegates’ PRESTO card or bank statement is required.

4.2 # Car (Personal/Rental)

  1. 4.2.1 # Driving may be used if it is less expensive per delegate than taking public transport.
  2. 4.2.2 # All drivers of the vehicle must have a valid driver’s license and be insured at all times.
  3. 4.2.3 # The usage of a private vehicle will be reimbursed at the CRA Kilometric Rate for Ontario that applies to the usage of personal vehicles for business purposes.
    1. 4.2.3.a # Proof of starting and ending odometer values must be provided in the form of a timestamped photograph.
  4. 4.2.4 # Rental cars should be booked at the lowest available rate for a standard vehicle, declining the rental agency’s insurance coverage if sufficient insurance is provided by the delegate’s credit card or an alternative insurance carrier.
    1. 4.2.4.a # All rental cars must be approved in advance by the President and the Director. The usage of a rental car is not a preferred mode of transport.
  5. 4.2.5 # All delegates travelling in a private or rental vehicle must sign the Society’s Waiver And Release From Liability for external transportation provided by the Society.

4.3 # Private Transport (Uber/Lyft/Taxi)

  1. 4.3.1 # These modes of transport are not preferred unless they are the most economical option.
  2. 4.3.2 # Delegates must take as few vehicles as possible by filling all the seats.

Reimbursement #

5.0 # General policies

  1. 5.0.1 # Delegates must be advised of the reimbursement conditions and be directed to review this section of the Policy no later than the first delegate meeting.
  2. 5.0.2 # Generally, transportation costs should be paid and reimbursed upon satisfactory completion of the conference.
  3. 5.0.3 # Generally, conference attendance and accommodation costs will be paid directly by the Society.
    1. 5.0.3.a # At the discretion of the Director and President, a delegate who fails to attend the conference may be asked to repay the conference fee to the Society.
  4. 5.0.4 # Transportation costs from the point of meeting in Toronto to the conference accommodation and back will be covered, provided that the delegate is present and is utilizing the transportation.
  5. 5.0.5 # Receipts are required for all expenses.
    1. 5.0.5.a # At the discretion of a signing Officer of the Society, this requirement may be waived for expenses under $10.00 CAD, such as transit fare, with proof of payment.
  6. 5.0.6 # All reimbursements will use the Society’s Cheque Requisition process.
  7. 5.0.7 # The Director shall update their internal budget after all the cheque requisitions have been processed for each conference.

Special Case Delegates #

6.0 # Day Pass Delegates

  1. 6.0.1 # Section 6.1 shall refer to all delegates selected for limited-access to conferences for a partial duration as specified by the organizing committee, on what is usually called a “Day Pass.”
  2. 6.0.2 # Generally, all sections apply to Day Pass Delegates, except they will arrange their own transportation to and from the conference host school for the partial duration of the conference in which they are attending.
  3. 6.0.3 # Transportation costs may still be reimbursed following guidelines outlined in Section 5.

6.1 # Delegates on Special Projects

  1. 6.1.1 # Section 6.2 shall refer to delegates who have special permission to attend a conference for the purpose of a special service to the conference or to the Society, including but not limited to holding a session as part of the conference on behalf of the Society or presenting a motion or bid at that conference’s General Meeting.
  2. 6.1.2 # Delegates who have a special reason to attend a conference must seek permission from the Director as soon as reasonably possible.
  3. 6.1.3 # Given the Delegate’s reason to attend the conference is approved, the Director must work with the VP Finance to get as much costs reasonably covered for the Delegate as possible.

6.2 # Competition Delegates

  1. 6.2.1 # Section 6.3 shall refer to all delegates who represent the Society at the Ontario Engineering Competition (“OEC”) hosted by the ESSCO and/or the Canadian Engineering Competition (“CEC”) hosted by the CFES.
  2. 6.2.2 # The list of Delegates to the OEC is not selected by the clauses outlined by Section 2. The University of Toronto Engineering Kompetitions (“UTEK”) Director, who oversees the Society’s internal qualifier for the OEC, will send the Director a list of Delegates who have been selected to attend.
  3. 6.2.3 # The list of Delegates to the CEC is not selected by the clauses outlined by Section 2. Qualifiers selected from the list of Delegates from the OEC shall automatically be on the list of Delegates selected to attend the CEC. These qualifiers are determined by the OEC Organizing Committee and will be communicated with the External Relations team as soon as possible following the conclusion of the OEC.
  4. 6.2.4 # Generally, Competition Delegates will still participate in activities outlined in Section 3, except Section 3.3 as the Competition will not require a Post-Conference Debrief. The waiving of the $50.00 CAD deposit may also be discussed with the Director and the President.
  5. 6.2.5 # The Ontario Ambassador (“OA”) oversees the delegation to the CEC. Should the Society be sending any Delegates to the CEC, the Director and/or President must maintain contact with the OA throughout the entire process from travel outside Toronto to their return to Toronto, as the OA is generally not a member of the Society.
